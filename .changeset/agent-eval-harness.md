Add `@uptimizr/agent-eval` (private, `oss/packages/agent-eval`): the evaluation harness for the analytics agent (ADR 0051 §8). A bank of 48 YAML questions spanning every registry category plus the three curated MCP prompts is asked against the seeded `@uptimizr/db` parity fixtures through the real collector — booted in-process and reached via `app.inject()` — and the generated read-tool catalog, then scored on tool selection, argument subset match and answer accuracy (numeric tolerance, required phrases, forbidden phrases). A deterministic scripted provider runs the whole bank in CI with no secret; a hosted provider (Anthropic `claude-sonnet-5` by default, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint) runs behind the `UPTIMIZR_EVAL_API_KEY` secret and is gated against a committed baseline; the WebLLM local-model leg is scaffolded on a weekly workflow. A coverage test fails the build when a served metric has no case and no written exemption. No published package changes.
